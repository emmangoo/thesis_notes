- Cancer cells acquire aberrantly active enhancers at oncogenic driver genes through diverse **genomic** (mutation, insertion, amplification, translocation, inversion, TAD-boundary disruption) and **epigenomic** (TF/cofactor dysregulation, oncovirus infection) mechanisms
- enhancer activation vs enhancer rewiring (=> changes which genes an enhancer contacts)
- enhancers can be rewired by changing the 3D genome architecture
- TADs are insulated domains formed by CTCF-CTCF loops reinforced by cohesin => TADs provide a physical boundary to enhancers reaching promoters
