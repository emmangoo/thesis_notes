"It is estimated that 15%–50% of all variants leading to disease in humans alter splicing."

"However, even with increasing precision, their accuracy remains imperfect, especially for diagnostics and for variants located far from the splice sites"

"the intron Jaccard index. It is defined as the proportion of reads supporting the splicing of an intron of interest among all reads associated with either splice site of the intron"

"the intron Jaccard index is computed from both split and non-split reads, thus allowing it to capture several types of aberrant splicing, including exon skipping, exon truncation, exon elongation, exon creation, and full intron retention"

"The possibility of focusing on candidate genes and variants in such a ‘‘DNA-first, RNAsecond’’ mode of operation can be valuable"

"One concern with moving from the original splicing metrics of FRASER to the intron Jaccard index is that some genuinely pathogenic isoforms can turn out to represent a minor fraction of the expressed splice isoforms and therefore could now be discarded."
