HCL-V = hairy cell leukemia variant 
A rare, malignant splenic B-cell lymphoma/leukemia characterized by circulating abnormal lymphocytes with intermediate morphology between prolymphocytes and hairy cells with positive expression of CD11c and negative expression of CD25, CD123 and the BRAFV600E mutation.

Myoproliferative neoplasm: <b>effective production</b> of mature, functioning myeloid cells. The bone marrow is hypercellular, and this overproduction is reflected in the peripheral blood.

Myelodysplastic neoplasm: <b>ineffective hematopoiesis</b>. While the bone marrow is often hypercellular (the body is trying to compensate), the cells produced are morphologically abnormal (dysplastic) and undergo premature apoptosis
